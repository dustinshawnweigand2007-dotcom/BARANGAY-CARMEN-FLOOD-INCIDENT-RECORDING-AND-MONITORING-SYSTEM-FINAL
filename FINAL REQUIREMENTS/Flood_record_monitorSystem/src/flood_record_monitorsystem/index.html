Services

Our flood incident recording and monitoring system provides essential tools to help Barangay Carmen organize, monitor, and manage flood-related information.

Flood Incident Recording

Record important information about flood incidents, including the date, location, water level, affected areas, and other relevant details.

Flood Monitoring

Monitor and review recorded flood information to help barangay personnel understand flood conditions and respond appropriately.

Affected Area Recording

Record locations and areas affected by flooding to help organize information and identify communities that may require assistance.

About Us

Our Mission

This project aims to create a reliable and efficient system for the documentation of flood incidents and related information for the staff of Barangay Carmen. This will ensure that the necessary records are created to guarantee the proper documentation and monitoring of flood incidents. The system will provide more effective data management so that personnel will be able to track the incidents, monitor the information recorded, and promote proper flood management in Barangay Carmen.

Our Vision

Our vision is to set up a flood monitoring system at Barangay Carmen that is dependable and orderly. This will allow the authorized Admin and Staff to accurately keep track of flood-related incidents. We envision a flood monitoring system that can keep the necessary records of past floods and assure good evaluation and coordination of flood incidents as well as the understanding of events and areas affected by the flooding.
